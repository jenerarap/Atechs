Empowering the Youth in STEM (2024-2025)

The Mission & Purpose

Serving as both Instructor and Project Manager at the Prizren Yunus Emre Institute, my primary goal was empowering the youth to enter STEM fields through robotics and engineering.

I designed a hands-on curriculum that moved beyond theory. We focused on Creative Problem Solving — teaching students not just how to wire a circuit, but why it works and how to apply it to solve real-world problems using Arduino, IoT, and Robotics.
