Robotic Arm

4-DOF Mechatronics Student Project

Project Overview

This project was successfully built by my students at the Prizren Yunus Emre Institute under my coordination.

The goal was to understand the kinematics of industrial robots. The students constructed a 4-Degree-of-Freedom (4-DOF) robotic arm capable of picking and placing objects with decent precision. It features manual control via potentiometers and can be programmed for automatic repetitive tasks.

View Project Link

Technical Specs

  • Controller: Arduino UNO
  • Actuators: 4x MG996R Servos
  • Input: Potentiometer
  • Axis: 4 Degrees of Freedom
  • Builders: Bonevet Students
