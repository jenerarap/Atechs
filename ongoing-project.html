Smart Hydroponics

Automated Indoor Grow System

The Build & Goal

Hydroponics is the science of growing plants without soil delivering a precise mix of water and nutrients directly to the roots. The ultimate goal of this project is to take that concept and fully automate it. I am engineering a smart, indoor hydroponics system optimized for leafy greens like lettuce, where custom microcontrollers seamlessly manage the entire artificial environment.

So far, the foundation is set. I’ve successfully built the core control unit to automate a strict 16 hour on / 8 hour off light cycle and regulated the pH levels of the water. I’ve also integrated a DHT11 sensor and an OLED display to actively monitor temperature and humidity inside the grow zone, giving me real time data to keep the plants in their optimal range.

Current Roadblocks

Engineering is rarely perfect on the first try. During my initial testing phase, I faced a major setback: my first batch of lettuce failed due to severe mold buildup. The enclosed environment trapped too much humidity without adequate airflow.

The Fix: I am currently redesigning the structural layout to incorporate an automated ventilation system that triggers when the DHT11 detects humidity spikes.

System Specs

  • Controller: Arduino Nano
  • Date: 13Feb 2026
  • Environment: DHT11 Sensor
  • UI: Live OLED Output
  • Actuators: 12V Pump & 5V Fan
  • Structure: 3D Printed Tower
