Smart Hydroponics

Automated Indoor Grow System

The Build & Goal

I am currently designing and building an automated indoor hydroponics setup specifically optimized to grow leafy greens like lettuce. The goal is to create a self sustaining system where environmental factors and light cycles are fully managed by custom microcontrollers.

So far, I have successfully engineered the core control unit. It utilizes an Arduino Nano paired with an RTC (Real Time Clock) module to dictate precise 16-hour on / 8-hour off light cycles. Additionally, I wired a DHT11 sensor and an OLED display to actively monitor the temperature and humidity levels inside the grow zone.

Current Roadblocks

Engineering is rarely perfect on the first try. During my initial testing phase, I faced a major setback: my first batch of lettuce failed due to severe mold buildup. The enclosed environment trapped too much humidity without adequate airflow.

The Fix: I am currently redesigning the structural layout to incorporate an automated ventilation system that triggers when the DHT11 detects humidity spikes.

Hardware Stack

  • RTC Module (Timing)
  • DHT11 Sensor
