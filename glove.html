Wireless Controller Glove

Gesture-Based Gaming & Remote Control

Project Overview

Traditional controllers restrict movement to just thumbs and fingers. I engineered this Wireless Controller Glove to map physical hand motions directly into digital inputs, replacing the need for a keyboard or standard joystick.

By utilizing an MPU6050 gyroscope and accelerometer, the glove precisely reads the roll of your hand. Tilt left or right to steer smoothly through corners, and use the integrated push buttons to trigger turbo and brakes. I specifically optimized and tested this build to play high speed racing games like Asphalt 8 perfectly.
